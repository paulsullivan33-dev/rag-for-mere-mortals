The lab — one small pipeline, three invented documents,
no model server
====================================================================

The lab is the pipeline of this guide, written with the
Python standard library only, on the build machine (an
Ubuntu 24.04 sandbox, 2 CPUs, 7.7 GB RAM):

  * Python 3.12.3. ragcore.py holds chunking and TF-IDF;
    chunk.py, build_index.py, search.py and ask.py are the
    four faces. Nothing was installed.
  * The documents are invented for the guide: a workshop
    manual for the "Bramble Mk II" bench polisher, a model
    boat club newsletter, and its regatta rules — 26 chunks
    in all, small enough that every number in the guide can
    be checked by hand.
  * The store is one SQLite file, rag.db, 122880 bytes:
    26 chunks, 437 terms, 658 postings.
  * There is no language model in the lab. The answering
    step is a mechanical stand-in that quotes a retrieved
    sentence or refuses; see chapter 9 for why, and for the
    documented-only swap that replaces it with a real
    model served by Ollama. A curl check for a model
    server (localhost, and one known serving address) is
    recorded in transcript t06: neither was reachable from
    the build machine.

What the lab ran, in rag-lab/transcripts/:

  t00  versions: Python 3.12.3, platform string
  t01  chunk.py over the three documents: 26 chunks
  t02  build_index.py, then the store read back with
       sqlite3 — counts, idf values, the belt postings
  t03  search.py on three questions: ranked chunks, scores
  t04  ask.py on six questions, prompts in full: four
       cited answers, two exact refusals
  t05  freshness: refused question, notice added, rebuild,
       same question answered from the new chunk
  t06  the model-server checks (both unreachable)
  t07  the nine-question test set of chapter 11: seven
       expected answers, two expected refusals, no
       invented answers

Calibration notes, kept because the guide quotes them:

  * The first version of the chunker made one-word chunks
    out of headings ("Dues", "Safety"). Headings of six
    words or fewer are now glued to the block that follows
    them (chapter 4).
  * The oil question initially retrieved the right chunk
    but quoted its "grease is forbidden" neighbour
    sentence — the word "use" in the question matched it.
    The manual's sentence was rewritten so the fact stands
    in one sentence; the incident is why chapter 11 scores
    answers instead of impressions.
