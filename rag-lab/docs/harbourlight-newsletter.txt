Harbourlight Model Boat Club
Newsletter, spring edition

From the secretary

Welcome to the spring edition. The lake
is clear of ice, the shed has a new roof,
and the sailing season opens this month.
This newsletter carries the dates for
your diary, the dues reminder, and news
of a new machine in the workshop.

Meetings and workshop nights

The club meets on the first Tuesday of
each month at 7.30 in the evening, in
the Long Room at the community hall.
Bring a boat or a work in progress; the
formal business rarely lasts past the
tea break. Workshop night is every
Thursday evening at the club shed by
the lake, from 6.30 until the light
goes or the kettle gives up.

Dues

Annual dues are 24 dollars for adults
and 8 dollars for juniors. Dues cover
the lake licence, the shed rent, and
insurance for club events. Please pay
the treasurer before the end of April
so the insurance list stays current.
New members joining after September pay
half dues for their first year.

The spring regatta

The spring regatta is on Saturday 14
June at Millpond Lake. Registration
opens at 9.30 in the morning and the
first race starts at 10.30. The full
rules are in the separate regatta rules
sheet, which the starter will have on
the day. Spectators are welcome, and
the tea tent will be open all day.

A new machine in the workshop

The club has bought a second-hand
Bramble Mk II bench polisher for the
workshop. It will polish propellers,
fittings, and anything else the fleet
brings in. The workshop manual for the
polisher is kept in the shed, on the
shelf above the bench. Please read the
safety page of the manual before your
first use, and leave the machine
unplugged when you finish.

Library corner

The club library has gained two shelves
of back issues of the modelling
magazines, donated by a member who is
moving abroad. Borrowing is free; write
your name and the date in the loans
book by the door. Books come back
within a month, please.

For sale and wanted

For sale: one unfinished hull, 900 mm,
free to a member who will finish it.
Wanted: offcuts of basswood for a
planking demonstration at the June
meeting. See the notice board in the
shed for both.
