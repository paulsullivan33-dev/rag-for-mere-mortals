Millpond Regatta Rules
Harbourlight Model Boat Club

Classes

Racing is in four classes: sail, steam,
electric fast, and electric scale. A
boat may enter one class only. The
starter decides which class a boat
belongs to, and the starter's decision
is final.

Registration and numbers

Every boat must carry a registration
number that can be read from the bank.
Numbers are issued at registration,
which opens at 9.30 in the morning.
Racing starts at 10.30. Late entries
are accepted only if a heat has a free
lane.

Safety on the pontoon

Children under 12 must wear a life
jacket while they are on the pontoon.
Only competitors and officials may go
beyond the rope during a race. Anyone
recovering a boat from the water uses
the long-handled net; nobody wades.

Radio control

Boats using older 27 MHz radio sets
must book a frequency peg from the
control board before switching on.
Modern 2.4 GHz sets need no peg. If two
boats find themselves on the same
frequency, both stop until the starter
sorts it out.

Prizes

Prizes are presented at 4 in the
afternoon on the bank by the tea tent.
There is a trophy for each class winner
and a separate prize for the best
home-built boat, judged during the
lunch break.
