[source: bramble-mk2-manual#5] Checking the belt: Check the belt tension every 40 hours of running. Press the belt midway between the pulleys with one finger. The belt should move 8 to 10 mm. If it moves more than 12 mm, slide the motor back on its rails until the tension is right, then tighten the two rail bolts. The drive belt is part number BP-114.
[source: bramble-mk2-manual#9] When something is wrong: If the polisher vibrates in use, first check that the wheel is balanced and that the bench bolts are tight. A wheel that has taken a knock can lose a chunk of felt and shake the whole bench.
[source: bramble-mk2-manual#4] Everyday use: Start the machine and let it reach full speed before you offer work to the wheel. Hold the piece with both hands and rest your forearms on the bench edge. Use a light touch: pressing hard slows the wheel, overheats the piece, and wears the felt pads out early. Work in short passes, and turn the piece often so the finish stays even.
[source: bramble-mk2-manual#2] About this machine: The Bramble Mk II is a bench polisher for small metal and wooden parts. A 370 watt motor drives two polishing wheels through a single belt. The no-load spindle speed is 2,950 rpm, the wheels are 150 mm in diameter, and the machine weighs 11 kg. It is built for a workshop bench, bolted down through the four holes in its base.
